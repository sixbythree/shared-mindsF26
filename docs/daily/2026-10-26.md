# 2026-10-26

## Done
- Started **100** (A05) in `a05_100/`: a social app capped at 100 connections where each connection has a temperature.
  - Settled the rules: everyone starts hot; temperature is mutual days in the last 7 (6–7 hot, 3–5 warm, 1–2 cold); a connection disappears only after 7 days with no engagement and both people are then blocked for 30 days. Engagement means an in-app call over 5 minutes or messaging, by both people. Days are New York calendar days. Kept the name "100" (considered "Temperature").
  - A full 100 means letting someone go to connect or accept.
  - Messages and WebRTC voice calls inside the app; posts (text, image, video link) with heat and comments, which don't count as engagement.
  - Person pages: clicking a name shows their posts, only if you're connected.
  - **Activities**: Watch (synced YouTube/.mp4; streaming apps open in a new tab with a host-run shared clock), Read (article list, who's on which article, shared notes), Study (shared focus timer, chat only on breaks). 5 minutes together counts as a shared day. Profiles show a person's current activity with Join.
  - Demo mode with ~140 LLM-backed fake people and a Next day button.
  - Logo: "1" plus two zeros drawn as eyes with lashes.
  - Firestore rules for all `hundred*` collections (in `firestore.rules`).
- Root index: 100 is card 03, Textual Charades moves to 04.
