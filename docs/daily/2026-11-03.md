- Built **Human Nature** (A08) in `a08_human_nature/`, a field of plants tended with the body.
  - Webcam + ml5 1.4.0: bodyPose (MoveNet) for arms, handPose for fingers. ml5 loads only when the camera starts.
  - Gestures: O over the head brings the sun (plants lean toward the screen and grow), finger flutter brings rain, a fast arm swing up throws seeds, a fast flat hand down chops the plant under it, a fist drags the view, a pinch opens a plant card.
  - 7 crops and 4 weeds, drawn procedurally. Crops grow in the sun, dry out without rain, flower, fruit, and can grow up barren (more likely if they went thirsty). Weeds grow on water alone, drink from neighbours and spread.
  - Keyboard fallback (O, R, S, C, drag, click), field saved in localStorage, `?speed=` to fast-forward.
  - Tested in headless Chrome (keyboard mode, and camera mode with a fake webcam: models load in about 5s) and with 14 synthetic-pose checks of the gesture logic in Node. Those checks found and fixed: a chop's wind-up counting as a throw, tucked-thumb fists not registering, false rain after opening a fist, and jitter keeping the rain on.
