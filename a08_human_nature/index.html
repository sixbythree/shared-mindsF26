Human Nature

A field is waiting for you. Your body brings the weather.

  • Make an O with your arms over your head and the sun comes out. The plants lean toward you and grow. Let go and they rest.
  • Show the backs of your hands and it rains. Without rain the field dries out.
  • Shake an OK sign and seeds fall from your fingers onto the field below. Pinch a weed or a plant that grew up without fruit to select it, then karate chop to cut it down.
  • Close your fist and pull to look around. Hold the OK sign still on a plant (a pinch) to learn what it is; pinch again to close the card.
  • Pinch a ripe plant to pick its fruit and get seeds back. Point one finger up to choose which seed you sow.

Sell your fruit at the market and save up for new farms with new views.

Stand back so the camera sees you from the waist up. Video stays on your computer.

Esc pauses. M shows you in the corner for recording. Keyboard: hold O for sun, R for rain, S throws seeds where the mouse is, click a plant to select it (and pick it when it's ripe), C cuts it, 1–7 choose a seed, drag to look around.
