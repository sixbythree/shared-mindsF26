Human Nature

A field is waiting for you. Your body brings the weather.

  • Make an O with your arms over your head and the sun comes out. The plants lean toward you and grow. Let go and they rest.
  • Drizzle with your fingers and it rains. Without rain the field dries out.
  • Throw to scatter seeds. Karate chop to cut down weeds and plants that grew up without fruit.
  • Close your fist and pull to look around. Pinch a plant to learn what it is.

Grow your plants until they bear fruit.

Stand back so the camera sees you from the waist up. Video stays on your computer.

Keyboard: hold O for sun, R for rain, S throws seeds and C chops where the mouse is, drag to look around, click a plant to name it.
