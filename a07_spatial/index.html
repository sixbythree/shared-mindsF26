Decisions

You start life at one year old, crawling. Things will appear in front of you. Go up to one and take it. The others disappear.

Something is judging every choice, and it won't tell you what it values. Some choices help you grow up, make you lighter and slow your aging. Some leave you stuck where you are, heavier, and aging faster. Some end everything.

Time passes either way. Watch your age against the clock.
